Abstract

Twenty years turning complex health and technology problems into solutions that ship.

Dr. Vinithasree Subbhuraam is a visionary leader in AI and digital transformation, with over twenty years of experience delivering high-impact AI/ML solutions and driving measurable business growth across technology sectors. She leads go-to-market strategy and forges partnerships with Fortune 500 companies and innovation hubs.

She is currently Founder of AI First Innovations, a consultancy translating complex real-world challenges into revenue-generating solutions using Generative and Agentic AI — and Founder of IMPACT Labs Academy, an EdTech venture teaching the next generation AI, critical thinking, and a sense of purpose.

She has published over 100 peer-reviewed journal articles and two books on disruptive solutions for global healthcare, and serves as Associate Editor for Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine.
